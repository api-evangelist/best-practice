We’re excited to launch our new Integrator Portal — a purpose-built tool to help streamline support and troubleshooting. The portal offers real-time visibility into the Halo connectivity status of your customer practices and offers the ability to send test queries directly from your subscription. Whether you're diagnosing connectivity issues or confirming a successful practice onboarding, the Integrator Portal makes it easy for your support teams to get the answers they need.
